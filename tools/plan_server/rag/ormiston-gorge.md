Ormiston Gorge is about 135 km west of Alice Springs and is accessed by sealed roads. It offers short walks, longer circuits, a waterhole and a campground. Water can be very cold and deep; do not jump or dive. Some routes may require walking through deep water, so check current walk conditions and offer a shorter alternative. Visitors need a NT Parks Visitor Pass; confirm park status, bookings and weather before travel.
