Leliyn is the northern visitor area of Nitmiluk National Park, about 60 km north of Katherine by sealed road. It has waterfalls, pools, walks and drive-in camping. A NT Parks Visitor Pass is required for visitors. Water and track access can change quickly with weather; check the live site status and swim only in designated areas shown open. Confirm campground bookings and facilities before arrival.
