Mindil Beach is a Larrakia and Tiwi place of significance. The Sunset Market is a seasonal dry-season event, typically Thursday and Sunday evenings from late April to late October; confirm dates and hours with the market before planning. The beach reserve also hosts community events, so check local notices. Do not infer natural-water swimming safety.
