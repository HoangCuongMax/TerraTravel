Angkerle Atwatye is a sacred Arrernte cultural place, 100% Aboriginal owned and operated. A short walking path leads to the chasm; entry fees apply. Conventional vehicles can reach the visitor facility during operating hours, but other West MacDonnell tracks may require high-clearance 4WD. Check the operator's current hours, fees and access information; respect local cultural guidance.
