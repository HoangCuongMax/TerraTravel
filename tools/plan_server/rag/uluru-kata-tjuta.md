A separate Parks Australia pass is required. The park offers designated viewing areas and walks at Uluṟu and Kata Tjuṯa. The Uluṟu climb is permanently closed; never recommend climbing. Respect Aṉangu Traditional Owners, sacred places, signage and no-photography sites. Check current park notices, walk access, pass details and forecast before planning.
