Darwin Waterfront is a short walk from the city. Its artificial beach and recreation lagoon provide managed saltwater swimming, with lifeguards at the beach. Weather can affect outdoor activities; check current notices and operating details before visiting. Do not generalise this managed facility's safety to Darwin's natural beaches or estuaries.
