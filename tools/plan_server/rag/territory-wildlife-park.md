About 50 km south of Darwin near Berry Springs, this park presents Top End wildlife and habitats along a 4 km loop, with shuttle trains, walking paths and animal presentations. Opening hours vary by season and the daily program can change. Check the official park page for current hours, entry and activities before planning.
