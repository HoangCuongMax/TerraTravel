Nitmiluk National Park is on Jawoyn Country. Its visitor areas are Nitmiluk Gorge (about 30 km northeast of Katherine) and Leliyn (Edith Falls, about 60 km north), separate road destinations. Gorge activities include walks, cruises and canoeing. A NT Parks Visitor Pass is required. Check status, weather, tours and swim guidance before travel; follow Jawoyn cultural guidance.
