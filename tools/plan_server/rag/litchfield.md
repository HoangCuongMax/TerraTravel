About 120 km southwest of Darwin, Litchfield has waterfalls, swimming areas, walks and magnetic termite mounds. Visitors need a NT Parks Visitor Pass. Main visitor areas and remote sites have different access; some tracks require 4WD. Do not route a standard car onto a 4WD-only track. Check the live park-status page, swimming guidance and NT Road Report before recommending a specific site.
