Alice Springs (Mparntwe) is a Red Centre service hub and gateway to desert parks and the MacDonnell Ranges. Confirm services directly. For outdoor activities, check the BOM forecast and NT heat guidance, carry water and plan strenuous walks for cooler periods. Check the NT Road Report before driving.
