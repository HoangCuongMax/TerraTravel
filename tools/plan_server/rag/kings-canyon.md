The Kings Canyon Rim Walk is a demanding loop with a steep initial climb. NT guidance requires walkers to start before 9am when the Watarrka forecast is 36°C or higher. Shorter walks are available. Check current park status and the BOM Watarrka forecast before planning. Bring water and suitable footwear; do not imply a walk is open or safe based on this static record.
