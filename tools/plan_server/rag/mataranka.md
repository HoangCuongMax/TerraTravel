The thermal pool is in Elsey National Park near Mataranka. The NT describes a short, accessible loop from the car park. Visitors need a NT Parks Visitor Pass. Swimming and road access can change, including during flooding; check the live Elsey park-status page and NT Road Report. Do not present swimming as available unless official status and on-site signage permit it.
