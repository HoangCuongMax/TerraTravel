Karlu Karlu is a jointly managed cultural landscape about 100 km south of Tennant Creek beside the Stuart Highway. Traditional Owners ask visitors not to climb the boulders. A NT Parks Visitor Pass is required. Camping needs an advance booking and fee; water and firewood are not supplied. Check access and bookings before travel.
