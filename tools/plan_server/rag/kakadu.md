Kakadu is a large, culturally significant park with visitor areas including Ubirr, Burrungkuy (Nourlangie), Yellow Water (Ngurrungurrudjba) and Bowali Visitor Centre. Park pass required; price and validity vary, so use the current Parks Australia pass page and do not quote a remembered fee. Access to sites and roads changes with season, flooding, fire and maintenance. Check the Kakadu Access Report and NT Road Report for each proposed site on the day. Some remote sites need high-clearance 4WD or a permit; do not assume a standard car can reach them. Follow crocodile warnings and swim only where park authorities explicitly allow it.
