The aviation complex is 3 km off the Stuart Highway; a key is available from the Daly Waters Pub. The Daly Waters Historic Pub offers backpacker, motel and cabin accommodation, a campground and a saltwater pool. Daly Waters is described as having Australia's first international airfield; the Daly Waters Aviation Complex is the oldest aviation structure in the NT. The pub was established in 1930 and licensed in 1938 to serve airfield passengers and crew.
