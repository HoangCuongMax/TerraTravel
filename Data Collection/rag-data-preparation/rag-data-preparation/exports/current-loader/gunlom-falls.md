The Gunlom access road is for high-clearance 4WD vehicles only. The Parks Australia page states there is strictly no access or swimming in the bottom plunge pool. Parks Australia pages have listed the lookout track as closed at the request of Traditional Owners until further notice; check the Kakadu Access Report. The Gunlom campground is first-come, first-served and the on-site manager collects fees. The Gunlom lookout walk is 0.5 km one way, about 30 minutes, with a short steep climb.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
