The park is on Larapinta Drive, 7 km from the centre of Alice Springs. There is a cafe, gift shop and picnic ground. Tourism NT lists opening daily 7:30am to 6pm and closure on Christmas Day; confirm on the park website. Highlights include the Nature Theatre free-flying birds of prey show, guided activities, cultural presentations and a nocturnal spotlight tour. Three habitats (sand country, desert rivers and woodland) hold native plants and wildlife.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
