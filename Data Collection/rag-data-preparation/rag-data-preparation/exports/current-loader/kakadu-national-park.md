Do not assume all of Kakadu is reachable by standard car; some remote attractions use rough or 4WD roads. Check the Kakadu Access Report first. Traditional Owners recognise six seasons; Parks Australia plans around a dry season (April to October) and tropical summer (November to March). Kakadu is crocodile country; swim only where signage and official status allow. Bowali Visitor Centre is near Jabiru. A park pass is required and is bought through Parks Australia. Size: 20000 km2 (approximate).
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
