Temperatures can exceed 40 C between October and April. About 135 km west of Alice Springs, per the NT Parks fact sheet. A parks pass applies to NT visitors. A campground is available. Pound walk: 8.5 km (Ormiston Pound Walk, 3 to 4 hours). Ormiston Gorge is the start of Sections 9 and 10 of the Larapinta Trail and the base for the Mount Giles overnight walk (Grade 5, difficult). The Aranda people call the ranges Tjoritja.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
