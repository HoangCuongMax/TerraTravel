Traditional Owners ask that the karlu (marbles) are not climbed. Temperatures across the NT can be very hot between October and April and can exceed 40 C. The reserve is 100 km south of Tennant Creek off the Stuart Highway. Visitors to the NT need a parks pass. Camping is available in the reserve. Karlu Karlu translates to 'round boulders'. Ownership was handed back to the traditional owners on 27 October 2008; the reserve is jointly managed with Parks and Wildlife rangers.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
