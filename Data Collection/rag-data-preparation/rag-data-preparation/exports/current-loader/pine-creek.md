The Mine Lookout is reached by a steep, narrow bitumen road that is not suitable for caravans. The Railway Station Museum has gold coin donation entry; check locally for opening times, and it is closed over the Top End summer season. The Northern Goldfields Loop is a heritage trail from Pine Creek to Adelaide River through Grove Hill past abandoned mines. The local Aboriginal people of the area are the Wagiman, Mayali, Arigoolia and Jawoyn. Pine Creek is at the junction of the Stuart and Kakadu Highways.
