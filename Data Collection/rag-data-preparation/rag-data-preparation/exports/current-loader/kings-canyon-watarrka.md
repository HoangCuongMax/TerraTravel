When the forecast is 36 C or above, the Rim Walk must start before 9am; no start is allowed after 9am. A heat management strategy applies. The Stuart and Lasseter Highways and Luritja Road are sealed and suitable for 2WD vehicles; unsealed roads may become impassable after rain. A Mereenie Loop Pass is needed on Larapinta Drive between Hermannsburg or Glen Helen and Watarrka. A parks pass is required for NT visitors. The Kings Canyon Rim Walk must be done clockwise and starts with a difficult climb, so it suits fit, healthy walkers. Kathleen Springs is 21 km by road from Kings Canyon.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
