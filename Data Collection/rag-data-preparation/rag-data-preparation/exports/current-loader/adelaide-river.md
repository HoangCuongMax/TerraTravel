A small Stuart Highway township that was a major Allied supply, communications and medical centre in WWII. The Adelaide River War Cemetery was established in mid 1942 and has two sections, one for service personnel and one for civilians.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
