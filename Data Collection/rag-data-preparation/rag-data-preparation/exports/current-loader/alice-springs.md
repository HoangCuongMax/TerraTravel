Spring and autumn are the best times to visit; December to February is low season. The airport is about 10 minutes from the town centre, and the Ghan train runs between Adelaide, Alice Springs and Darwin. Three bus routes run Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays. Alice Springs is Mparntwe to the Arrernte people, its Traditional Custodians. Aboriginal art galleries line the Desert Art Trail.
