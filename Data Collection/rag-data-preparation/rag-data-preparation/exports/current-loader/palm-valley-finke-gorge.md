The Palm Valley access road and campground are accessible to high-clearance 4WD only, recommended for experienced 4WD drivers only, with deep sand and river crossings. The Finke River 4WD route (Boggy Hole track) through to Illamurta Springs and onto Watarrka is much more remote. Camping is only permitted at the designated areas at Palm Valley and Boggy Hole; fires only in designated fire pits; pets are not permitted in the park. Finke Gorge Ranger Station phone: 08 8956 7401. Area: 46000 ha.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
