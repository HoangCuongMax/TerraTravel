Katherine is on the lands where the Jawoyn, Dagoman and Wardaman peoples meet. Route towns: Adelaide River, Burrundie, Pine Creek. Town base for Nitmiluk Gorge and Leliyn (Edith Falls). Katherine Hot Springs is a short drive from town on the southern side of the Stuart Highway.
