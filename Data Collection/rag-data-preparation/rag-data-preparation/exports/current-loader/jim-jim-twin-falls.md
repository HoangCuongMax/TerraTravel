Access is by 4WD only for Jim Jim Falls; reaching Twin Falls needs a high-clearance 4WD with a snorkel because of the Jim Jim Creek crossing. Parks Australia has closed this area for whole seasons or years for road and infrastructure works; always check the Kakadu Access Report. In the tropical summer the area is inaccessible by road; the falls can be seen by air. Dry season access is by 4WD. The Twin Falls plateau walk is a challenging 6 km return hike with views down into the gorge. Height: 200 m (Jim Jim Falls).
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
