Dry season (May to October) is high season; the wet season brings storms and fewer crowds. Airport distance to city: 7 km. Many attractions can be reached on foot or by bike, and buses serve popular tourist spots. The Larrakia people are the Traditional Custodians of Darwin (Garramilla). Capital of the Northern Territory. Aboriginal art appears in galleries, museums and streetscapes; there are night markets and an Asian-influenced food scene.
