The Uluṟu climb closed permanently on 26 October 2019. Never recommend climbing Uluṟu. Visit in ways that respect Tjukurpa (Aṉangu law and Culture), and follow Aṉangu cultural guidance and park signage. The park can close temporarily; confirm entry requirements, opening times and walk closures on uluru.gov.au. The park was handed back to its Traditional Owners, Aṉangu, in 1985.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
