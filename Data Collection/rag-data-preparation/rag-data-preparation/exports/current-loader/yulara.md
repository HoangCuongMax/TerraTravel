Ayers Rock Airport is a few kilometres from the resort, with daily Qantas flights to several capitals and Alice Springs. Yulara has a commercial centre and supermarket, bank, newsagent, food outlets, a service station, a Royal Flying Doctor Service clinic, police and fire service. Accommodation ranges from hotel and apartment style to budget and camping. Ayers Rock Campground is the only campground in the accommodation precinct, 20 km from Uluṟu; free camping is not permitted in or around the park. Ayers Rock Resort is an Aboriginal-owned enterprise offering tours and cultural experiences.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
