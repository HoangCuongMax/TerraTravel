Listed hours are 8am to 5pm with last entry at 4:30pm; confirm with the operator. A cafe and a campground are listed. Walk: 2.4 km return (easy self-guided walk). Entry adult: 12 AUD (indicative adult entry; confirm before travel). Angkerle Atwatye ('Gap of Water') is on a private reserve owned by the Iwupataka Land Trust and is culturally important to Western Arrernte women.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
