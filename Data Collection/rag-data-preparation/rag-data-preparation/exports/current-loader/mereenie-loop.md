NT Parks recommends a 4WD to travel the Red Centre Way via Larapinta Drive through the West MacDonnell Ranges. Unsealed roads may become impassable after rain. The Mereenie Loop is the unsealed section of the Red Centre Way between Kings Canyon Resort and the Larapinta/Namatjira Drive intersection. A Mereenie Loop Pass is needed on Larapinta Drive between Hermannsburg or Glen Helen and Watarrka National Park. Tourism Central Australia lists the permit at $5.00, valid for 3 days from purchase; confirm before travel.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
