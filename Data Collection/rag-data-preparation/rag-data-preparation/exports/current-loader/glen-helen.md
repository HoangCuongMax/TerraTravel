The Tourism NT listing describes Glen Helen as the only accommodation inside Tjoritja, with 25 motel rooms and powered and unpowered camping. The listing describes a restaurant, bar/bistro, swimming pool and Optus 4G mobile access at the property. The listing states the property is temporarily closed (page updated 7 August 2025); confirm before relying on fuel, food or beds here. Western Aranda people have lived in this landscape for tens of thousands of years.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
