The park can close due to bushfire, flooding or maintenance; NT Parks advises checking which swimming spots are open. Temperatures can exceed 40 C between October and April. The Gorge entrance is north-east of Katherine on a sealed road; Leliyn (Edith Falls) is a separate section north of Katherine, also on a sealed road. A parks pass is required for NT visitors. The Nitmiluk Visitor Centre at the Gorge entrance is the suggested first stop. List: Walks, Gorge boat tours, Jatbula Trail.
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
