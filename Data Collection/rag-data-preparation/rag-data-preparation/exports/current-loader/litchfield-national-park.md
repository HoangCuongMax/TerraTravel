The Lost City and Reynolds Track are 4WD routes. NT Parks lists Greenant Creek, Tolmer Falls and The Lost City as no swimming. The park can close for bushfire, flooding or maintenance; check the official status page and swim only where signage and status say it is open. Reached via Cox Peninsula Road or via Batchelor. Visitors to the NT need an NT Parks Visitor Pass. Drone flying needs a permit before arrival. Important to the Koongurrukun, Mak Mak Marranunggu, Werat and Warray Aboriginal people. Area: 1500 km2 (approximate).
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
