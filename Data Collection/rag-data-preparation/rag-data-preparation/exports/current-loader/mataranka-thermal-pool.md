Riverside walks have been closed for seasonal conditions on the official status page, and sites can close for fire or flooding. The pool is reached via Homestead Road; Bitter Springs is reached via Martin Road. A parks pass is required. A path runs from the car park to the pool, with stairs and handrails into the water, and a 500 m loop walk. Camping is at Jalmurark Campground; there is no camping at Bitter Springs. Water temperature: 34 °C (thermal pool and Bitter Springs, year round).
Closures, fuel, coverage and bookings are not stored here: check official sources before you go.
