# Leliyn (Edith Falls)

## Getting there and access
- Reached on a sealed road: turn off the Stuart Highway north of Katherine, then a further 20 km to the park entry. [leliyn-edith-falls.access.road_surface]

## Facilities and stays
- A kiosk near the car park sells food and drinks; picnic tables, gas barbecues, toilets and showers are nearby. [leliyn-edith-falls.facilities.kiosk_and_amenities]
- Two camping options are available; payment cannot be made at the park. [leliyn-edith-falls.facilities.camping]

## Permits and passes
- A parks pass is required. [leliyn-edith-falls.permits.park_pass]

## Activities
- Walk upper pools: 2.6 km return. [leliyn-edith-falls.activities.walk_upper_pools]
- Walk sweetwater: 8.6 km return. [leliyn-edith-falls.activities.walk_sweetwater]

## Safety and seasons
- Rangers monitor swimming holes. The official status page has previously listed the main plunge pool as closed after flood damage, so swim only where current signage and the status page say it is open. [leliyn-edith-falls.safety.swimming_status]
