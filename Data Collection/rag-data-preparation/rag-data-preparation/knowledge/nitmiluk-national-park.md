# Nitmiluk National Park

## Getting there and access
- The Gorge entrance is north-east of Katherine on a sealed road; Leliyn (Edith Falls) is a separate section north of Katherine, also on a sealed road. [nitmiluk-national-park.access.road_surface]

## Facilities and stays
- The Nitmiluk Visitor Centre at the Gorge entrance is the suggested first stop. [nitmiluk-national-park.facilities.visitor_centre]

## Permits and passes
- A parks pass is required for NT visitors. [nitmiluk-national-park.permits.park_pass]

## Activities
- List: Walks, Gorge boat tours, Jatbula Trail. [nitmiluk-national-park.activities.list]

## Safety and seasons
- Temperatures can exceed 40 C between October and April. [nitmiluk-national-park.safety.heat]
- The park can close due to bushfire, flooding or maintenance; NT Parks advises checking which swimming spots are open. [nitmiluk-national-park.safety.closures_pattern]

## Culture and country
- The park is on the lands of the Jawoyn people, with rock art sites across the park; Nitmiluk is the Jawoyn name for Katherine Gorge. [nitmiluk-national-park.culture.traditional_owners]
