# Gunlom Falls

Closures, fuel prices, coverage and booking availability are not stored here; check official live sources before travel.

## Getting there and access
- The Gunlom access road is for high-clearance 4WD vehicles only. [gunlom-falls.access.vehicle_4wd]

## Facilities and stays
- The Gunlom campground is first-come, first-served and the on-site manager collects fees. [gunlom-falls.facilities.camping]

## Indicative prices
- Listed rates were adults $15.00, children $7.50, families $38.00 per night; confirm before travel. [gunlom-falls.prices.camping_nightly]

## Activities
- The Gunlom lookout walk is 0.5 km one way, about 30 minutes, with a short steep climb. [gunlom-falls.activities.lookout_walk]

## Safety and seasons
- The Parks Australia page states there is strictly no access or swimming in the bottom plunge pool. [gunlom-falls.safety.no_swimming_bottom_pool]
- Parks Australia pages have listed the lookout track as closed at the request of Traditional Owners until further notice; check the Kakadu Access Report. [gunlom-falls.safety.closures_pattern]
