# Kakadu National Park

Closures, fuel prices, coverage and booking availability are not stored here; check official live sources before travel.

## Overview
- Size: 20000 km2 (approximate). [kakadu-national-park.overview.size]
- Kakadu is a dual-listed UNESCO World Heritage area with world-class rock art, wetlands and Stone Country. [kakadu-national-park.overview.heritage]

## Getting there and access
- Bowali Visitor Centre is near Jabiru. [kakadu-national-park.access.visitor_centre]
- Do not assume all of Kakadu is reachable by standard car; some remote attractions use rough or 4WD roads. Check the Kakadu Access Report first. [kakadu-national-park.access.vehicle_scope]

## Permits and passes
- A park pass is required and is bought through Parks Australia. [kakadu-national-park.permits.park_pass]

## Safety and seasons
- Traditional Owners recognise six seasons; Parks Australia plans around a dry season (April to October) and tropical summer (November to March). [kakadu-national-park.safety.seasons]
- Kakadu is crocodile country; swim only where signage and official status allow. [kakadu-national-park.safety.crocodiles]
