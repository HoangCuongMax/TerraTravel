# Jim Jim Falls and Twin Falls

## Overview
- Height: 200 m (Jim Jim Falls). [jim-jim-twin-falls.overview.height]

## Getting there and access
- Access is by 4WD only for Jim Jim Falls; reaching Twin Falls needs a high-clearance 4WD with a snorkel because of the Jim Jim Creek crossing. [jim-jim-twin-falls.access.vehicle_4wd]

## Activities
- The Twin Falls plateau walk is a challenging 6 km return hike with views down into the gorge. [jim-jim-twin-falls.activities.twin_falls_plateau]

## Safety and seasons
- In the tropical summer the area is inaccessible by road; the falls can be seen by air. Dry season access is by 4WD. [jim-jim-twin-falls.safety.seasonal_access]
- Parks Australia has closed this area for whole seasons or years for road and infrastructure works; always check the Kakadu Access Report. [jim-jim-twin-falls.safety.closures_pattern]
