## Getting there and access
- The reserve is 100 km south of Tennant Creek off the Stuart Highway. [karlu-karlu-devils-marbles.access.location]

## Facilities and stays
- Camping is available in the reserve. [karlu-karlu-devils-marbles.facilities.camping]

## Permits and passes
- Visitors to the NT need a parks pass. [karlu-karlu-devils-marbles.permits.park_pass]

## Safety and seasons
- Temperatures across the NT can be very hot between October and April and can exceed 40 C. [karlu-karlu-devils-marbles.safety.heat]

## Culture and country
- Karlu Karlu translates to 'round boulders'. [karlu-karlu-devils-marbles.culture.name]
- Traditional Owners ask that the karlu (marbles) are not climbed. [karlu-karlu-devils-marbles.culture.rules]
- Ownership was handed back to the traditional owners on 27 October 2008; the reserve is jointly managed with Parks and Wildlife rangers. [karlu-karlu-devils-marbles.culture.joint_management]
