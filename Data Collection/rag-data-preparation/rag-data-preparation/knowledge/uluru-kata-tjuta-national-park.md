## Safety and seasons
- The park can close temporarily; confirm entry requirements, opening times and walk closures on uluru.gov.au. [uluru-kata-tjuta-national-park.safety.closures_pattern]

## Culture and country
- The Uluṟu climb closed permanently on 26 October 2019. Never recommend climbing Uluṟu. [uluru-kata-tjuta-national-park.culture.climb_closure]
- The park was handed back to its Traditional Owners, Aṉangu, in 1985. [uluru-kata-tjuta-national-park.culture.handback]
- Visit in ways that respect Tjukurpa (Aṉangu law and Culture), and follow Aṉangu cultural guidance and park signage. [uluru-kata-tjuta-national-park.culture.rules]
