## Overview
- Area: 46000 ha. [palm-valley-finke-gorge.overview.area]

## Getting there and access
- The Palm Valley access road and campground are accessible to high-clearance 4WD only, recommended for experienced 4WD drivers only, with deep sand and river crossings. [palm-valley-finke-gorge.access.vehicle_4wd]
- The Finke River 4WD route (Boggy Hole track) through to Illamurta Springs and onto Watarrka is much more remote. [palm-valley-finke-gorge.access.finke_route]

## Facilities and stays
- Camping is only permitted at the designated areas at Palm Valley and Boggy Hole; fires only in designated fire pits; pets are not permitted in the park. [palm-valley-finke-gorge.facilities.camping_rules]
- Finke Gorge Ranger Station phone: 08 8956 7401. [palm-valley-finke-gorge.facilities.ranger_contact]
