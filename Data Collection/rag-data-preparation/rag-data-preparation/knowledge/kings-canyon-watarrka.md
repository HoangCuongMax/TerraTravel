## Getting there and access
- The Stuart and Lasseter Highways and Luritja Road are sealed and suitable for 2WD vehicles; unsealed roads may become impassable after rain. [kings-canyon-watarrka.access.roads]

## Permits and passes
- A Mereenie Loop Pass is needed on Larapinta Drive between Hermannsburg or Glen Helen and Watarrka. [kings-canyon-watarrka.permits.mereenie_pass]
- A parks pass is required for NT visitors. [kings-canyon-watarrka.permits.park_pass]

## Activities
- The Kings Canyon Rim Walk must be done clockwise and starts with a difficult climb, so it suits fit, healthy walkers. [kings-canyon-watarrka.activities.rim_walk]
- Kathleen Springs is 21 km by road from Kings Canyon. [kings-canyon-watarrka.activities.kathleen_springs]
- The Garden of Eden cannot be reached from the South Wall Return Walk. [kings-canyon-watarrka.activities.garden_of_eden]

## Safety and seasons
- When the forecast is 36 C or above, the Rim Walk must start before 9am; no start is allowed after 9am. A heat management strategy applies. [kings-canyon-watarrka.safety.heat_rule]
