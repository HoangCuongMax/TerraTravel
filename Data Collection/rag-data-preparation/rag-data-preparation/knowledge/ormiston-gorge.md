# Ormiston Gorge

## Getting there and access
- About 135 km west of Alice Springs, per the NT Parks fact sheet. [ormiston-gorge.access.location]

## Facilities and stays
- A campground is available. [ormiston-gorge.facilities.camping]

## Permits and passes
- A parks pass applies to NT visitors. [ormiston-gorge.permits.park_pass]

## Activities
- Pound walk: 8.5 km (Ormiston Pound Walk, 3 to 4 hours). [ormiston-gorge.activities.pound_walk]
- Ormiston Gorge is the start of Sections 9 and 10 of the Larapinta Trail and the base for the Mount Giles overnight walk (Grade 5, difficult). [ormiston-gorge.activities.trail_context]

## Safety and seasons
- Temperatures can exceed 40 C between October and April. [ormiston-gorge.safety.heat]

## Culture and country
- The Aranda people call the ranges Tjoritja. [ormiston-gorge.culture.name]
