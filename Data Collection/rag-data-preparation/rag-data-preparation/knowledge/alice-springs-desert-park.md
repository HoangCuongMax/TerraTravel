# Alice Springs Desert Park

## Overview
- Three habitats (sand country, desert rivers and woodland) hold native plants and wildlife. [alice-springs-desert-park.overview.habitats]

## Getting there and access
- The park is on Larapinta Drive, 7 km from the centre of Alice Springs. [alice-springs-desert-park.access.location]

## Facilities and stays
- There is a cafe, gift shop and picnic ground. [alice-springs-desert-park.facilities.visitor]
- Tourism NT lists opening daily 7:30am to 6pm and closure on Christmas Day; confirm on the park website. [alice-springs-desert-park.facilities.opening_hours]

## Activities
- Highlights include the Nature Theatre free-flying birds of prey show, guided activities, cultural presentations and a nocturnal spotlight tour. [alice-springs-desert-park.activities.shows]
