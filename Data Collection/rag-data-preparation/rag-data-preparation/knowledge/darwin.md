## Overview
- Capital of the Northern Territory. [darwin.overview.designation]
- Aboriginal art appears in galleries, museums and streetscapes; there are night markets and an Asian-influenced food scene. [darwin.overview.culture]

## Getting there and access
- Airport distance to city: 7 km. [darwin.access.airport_distance_to_city]
- Many attractions can be reached on foot or by bike, and buses serve popular tourist spots. [darwin.access.getting_around]

## Safety and seasons
- Dry season (May to October) is high season; the wet season brings storms and fewer crowds. [darwin.safety.seasonal_pattern]

## Culture and country
- The Larrakia people are the Traditional Custodians of Darwin (Garramilla). [darwin.culture.traditional_custodians]
