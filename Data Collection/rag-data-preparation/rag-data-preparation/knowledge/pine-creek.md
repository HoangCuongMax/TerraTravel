## Overview
- Pine Creek is at the junction of the Stuart and Kakadu Highways. [pine-creek.overview.location]

## Getting there and access
- The Mine Lookout is reached by a steep, narrow bitumen road that is not suitable for caravans. [pine-creek.access.mine_lookout]

## Activities
- The Railway Station Museum has gold coin donation entry; check locally for opening times, and it is closed over the Top End summer season. [pine-creek.activities.railway_museum]
- The Northern Goldfields Loop is a heritage trail from Pine Creek to Adelaide River through Grove Hill past abandoned mines. [pine-creek.activities.goldfields_loop]

## Culture and country
- The local Aboriginal people of the area are the Wagiman, Mayali, Arigoolia and Jawoyn. [pine-creek.culture.traditional_custodians]
