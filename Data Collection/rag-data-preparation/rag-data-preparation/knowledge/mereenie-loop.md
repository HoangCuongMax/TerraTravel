## Getting there and access
- The Mereenie Loop is the unsealed section of the Red Centre Way between Kings Canyon Resort and the Larapinta/Namatjira Drive intersection. [mereenie-loop.access.road_surface]
- NT Parks recommends a 4WD to travel the Red Centre Way via Larapinta Drive through the West MacDonnell Ranges. [mereenie-loop.access.vehicle_4wd]

## Permits and passes
- A Mereenie Loop Pass is needed on Larapinta Drive between Hermannsburg or Glen Helen and Watarrka National Park. [mereenie-loop.permits.mereenie_pass]
- Tourism Central Australia lists the permit at $5.00, valid for 3 days from purchase; confirm before travel. [mereenie-loop.permits.cost_validity]

## Safety and seasons
- Unsealed roads may become impassable after rain. [mereenie-loop.safety.unsealed_after_rain]
