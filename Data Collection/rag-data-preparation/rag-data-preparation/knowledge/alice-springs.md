## Overview
- Aboriginal art galleries line the Desert Art Trail. [alice-springs.overview.art]

## Getting there and access
- The airport is about 10 minutes from the town centre, and the Ghan train runs between Adelaide, Alice Springs and Darwin. [alice-springs.access.transport]
- Three bus routes run Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays. [alice-springs.access.buses]

## Safety and seasons
- Spring and autumn are the best times to visit; December to February is low season. [alice-springs.safety.seasonal_pattern]

## Culture and country
- Alice Springs is Mparntwe to the Arrernte people, its Traditional Custodians. [alice-springs.culture.traditional_custodians]
