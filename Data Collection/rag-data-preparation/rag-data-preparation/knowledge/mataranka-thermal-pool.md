# Mataranka Thermal Pool

Closures, fuel prices, coverage and booking availability are not stored here; check official live sources before travel.

## Overview
- Water temperature: 34 °C (thermal pool and Bitter Springs, year round). [mataranka-thermal-pool.overview.water_temperature]

## Getting there and access
- The pool is reached via Homestead Road; Bitter Springs is reached via Martin Road. [mataranka-thermal-pool.access.routes]

## Facilities and stays
- A path runs from the car park to the pool, with stairs and handrails into the water, and a 500 m loop walk. [mataranka-thermal-pool.facilities.pool_access]
- Camping is at Jalmurark Campground; there is no camping at Bitter Springs. [mataranka-thermal-pool.facilities.camping]

## Permits and passes
- A parks pass is required. [mataranka-thermal-pool.permits.park_pass]

## Safety and seasons
- Riverside walks have been closed for seasonal conditions on the official status page, and sites can close for fire or flooding. [mataranka-thermal-pool.safety.closures_pattern]
