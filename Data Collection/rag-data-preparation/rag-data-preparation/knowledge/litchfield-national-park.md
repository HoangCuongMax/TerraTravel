# Litchfield National Park

Entity: `litchfield-national-park` · Region: top_end · Retrieved: 2026-10-17 · Status: unverified (no independent review yet)

Closures, fuel prices, coverage and booking availability are not stored here; check official live sources before travel.

## Overview
- Area: 1500 km2 (approximate). [litchfield-national-park.overview.area]

## Getting there and access
- Reached via Cox Peninsula Road or via Batchelor. [litchfield-national-park.access.entry_routes]
- The Lost City and Reynolds Track are 4WD routes. [litchfield-national-park.access.vehicle_4wd]

## Facilities and stays
- Named sites: Wangi Falls, Florence Falls, Buley Rockhole, Cascades, Tolmer Falls, Walker Creek, The Lost City, Reynolds Track. [litchfield-national-park.facilities.named_sites]

## Permits and passes
- Visitors to the NT need an NT Parks Visitor Pass. [litchfield-national-park.permits.park_pass]
- Drone flying needs a permit before arrival. [litchfield-national-park.permits.drones]

## Safety and seasons
- NT Parks lists Greenant Creek, Tolmer Falls and The Lost City as no swimming. [litchfield-national-park.safety.no_swimming_sites]
- The park can close for bushfire, flooding or maintenance; check the official status page and swim only where signage and status say it is open. [litchfield-national-park.safety.closures_pattern]

## Culture and country
- Important to the Koongurrukun, Mak Mak Marranunggu, Werat and Warray Aboriginal people. [litchfield-national-park.culture.traditional_owners]
