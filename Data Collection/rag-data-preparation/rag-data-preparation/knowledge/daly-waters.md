## Overview
- Daly Waters is described as having Australia's first international airfield; the Daly Waters Aviation Complex is the oldest aviation structure in the NT. [daly-waters.overview.airfield]
- The pub was established in 1930 and licensed in 1938 to serve airfield passengers and crew. [daly-waters.overview.pub_history]

## Getting there and access
- The aviation complex is 3 km off the Stuart Highway; a key is available from the Daly Waters Pub. [daly-waters.access.aviation_complex]

## Facilities and stays
- The Daly Waters Historic Pub offers backpacker, motel and cabin accommodation, a campground and a saltwater pool. [daly-waters.facilities.pub_accommodation]
