## Getting there and access
- Ayers Rock Airport is a few kilometres from the resort, with daily Qantas flights to several capitals and Alice Springs. [yulara.access.airport]

## Facilities and stays
- Yulara has a commercial centre and supermarket, bank, newsagent, food outlets, a service station, a Royal Flying Doctor Service clinic, police and fire service. [yulara.facilities.services]
- Accommodation ranges from hotel and apartment style to budget and camping. [yulara.facilities.accommodation]
- Ayers Rock Campground is the only campground in the accommodation precinct, 20 km from Uluṟu; free camping is not permitted in or around the park. [yulara.facilities.camping]

## Culture and country
- Ayers Rock Resort is an Aboriginal-owned enterprise offering tours and cultural experiences. [yulara.culture.ownership]
